# Privacy notice template (India, DPDP Act 2023)

> **For the business:** replace everything in `[brackets]`, delete what doesn't apply, and have
> it reviewed by a lawyer. This is a starting point, not legal advice. Publish it at a stable URL
> and set that URL in Avery's Settings → Privacy & data. Rule 3 of the DPDP Rules 2025 asks for a
> notice that is understandable on its own, in clear and plain language, and lists each item of
> personal data with its purpose. You may also need to offer it in English and the languages in
> the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution that your callers use.

---

## Privacy notice — [Business name]

Last updated: [date]

[Business name] ("we") uses an AI phone and WhatsApp assistant to answer calls and messages,
answer questions, book appointments and take messages for our staff. This notice explains what
personal data we collect when you contact us, why, and your rights under the Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023.

### What we collect and why

| Personal data | Why we use it |
|---------------|---------------|
| Your phone number | To recognise you when you call or message again, show your bookings, and send booking confirmations. We store a coded (hashed) version to link your calls; your number itself is kept only on bookings and messages. |
| What you say on the call, or write on WhatsApp (as a written transcript) | To understand and handle your request, and so our staff can check what was agreed. |
| Your name, [vehicle / reason for visit / other details], and callback number | To make and manage your booking, or pass your message to our staff. |
| Booking details (service, date and time) | To provide the service and keep our records. |
| A record that you heard this notice (time, call reference) | To show that we told you how your data is used. |

We do **not** record or keep the audio of your call. We do not use your data for advertising,
and we do not sell it.

### AI assistant

Calls and messages are answered by an AI assistant, not a person. You can ask to leave a message
for, or be transferred to, a member of our staff [during opening hours].

### Who processes your data for us

We use service providers that process your data on our behalf and under our instructions:
[Avery / operator name] (the assistant), Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Twilio [and/or Exotel]
(telephony and text messages), Deepgram (speech-to-text), Cartesia (text-to-speech), Anthropic
(the AI model), Meta (WhatsApp)[, Google (calendar, if connected)][, Resend (email to our staff)].
Some of these providers process data outside India.

### How long we keep it

- Call and chat transcripts and messages for our staff: [N] days, then deleted automatically.
- Booking records: [period, e.g. as required for our accounts]. If you ask us to erase your data,
  we remove your name and contact details from them.
- The record that you heard this notice: at least one year.

### Your rights

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it (and get a copy);
- correct or update it;
- erase it, where we don't need to keep it by law;
- nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it; this doesn't affect what we did
before.

To make a request or a complaint, contact [name / Grievance Officer] at [email] or [phone]. We may
call you back on your number to confirm it's you. We will respond within [N, at most 90] days.
If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
