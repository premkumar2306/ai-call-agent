## 3. What the market says

1. **Pluxo is the benchmark, not a footnote.** It publishes ₹2,999/month for 250 AI-call minutes, unlimited WhatsApp, booking, reminders, recalls and 11 Indian languages. At 600 minutes it lands near Avery's current bill. Avery cannot claim to be the only turnkey Indian clinic receptionist.
2. **Practo is an incumbent workflow and a channel, not the product to replace.** Ray owns the clinic diary; Ray Connect manages calls but is not an AI receptionist. Ringg's Practo case study proves Practo is already willing to use external voice AI for booking at scale.
3. **Raw infrastructure can look cheaper.** Ringg is ₹6/connected minute including telephony; Bolna starts at ₹5.52/minute but its documentation says telephony/provider components can vary. Agencies can assemble a cheaper-looking demo, but the clinic still owns integration, testing and operations.
4. **WhatsApp products set a ₹1,500–₹3,200 entry anchor.** AiSensy, Interakt and WATI solve messaging, not unanswered calls. Lead with recovered calls; present WhatsApp as the confirmation and retention layer.
5. **Global turnkey products validate willingness to pay but are poor India fits.** Rosie, Goodcall and Smith.ai are materially more expensive after conversion and do not publish India numbers, Hindi/Hinglish or Indian WhatsApp workflows.

## 4. Positioning

### Avery's real differentiators

- **Deploy on Exotel or Plivo with DPDP disclosure/consent records and Razorpay GST billing.** Those operational details are already productized, while platforms leave them to the implementer.
- **One owner product across phone and WhatsApp.** Calls, booking, reminders and missed-call follow-up share business data and the same dashboard.
- **General-business breadth.** Avery can serve dental, medical, legal and appointment businesses; Pluxo is healthcare-only. This matters for agencies and small groups with mixed businesses, but is not a dental-clinic buying advantage.
- **Transparent Google Calendar path.** This is simple for clinics without a PMS and easier to explain than a custom integration project.

### Avery's real gaps

- Pluxo publishes 11 Indian languages; Avery has English, Hindi and Hinglish.
- Pluxo publishes recalls, no-show recovery, reviews and patient records; Avery's clinic workflow is thinner.
- Arini demonstrates the value of direct dental-PMS integration; Avery only has Google Calendar.
- Avery has no published Indian dental outcomes or clinic logos.

### Who to avoid head-on

- **Do not fight Pluxo on language count or healthcare workflow today.** Win on transparent onboarding, DPDP evidence, call quality, Google Calendar simplicity and a measured pilot; close feature gaps quickly.
- **Do not replace Practo/Clinicia/Eka.** Position Avery as the voice-and-WhatsApp layer that feeds or complements the clinic's existing diary.
- **Do not bid against Ringg/Bolna on enterprise per-minute price.** Focus on single clinics and small chains that want an operating product, not an agent-building platform.
- **Do not sell as a cheaper WhatsApp bot.** AiSensy/Interakt/WATI will always anchor lower because they do not answer calls.

## 5. Recommended Indian dental pricing

| Plan | Price before GST | Included | Overage |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Starter | ₹2,999/month | 300 min, one number, confirmations and missed-call follow-up | ₹7/min |
| **Clinic (default)** | **₹4,999/month** | **800 min, one number, reminders, transfers and onboarding** | **₹6/min** |
| Small chain | ₹9,999/month | 2,000 min, up to three numbers | ₹5/min |

**Why ₹4,999:** it makes the 600-minute bill predictable; undercuts Avery's current metered bill; includes 200 minutes of headroom; and is approximately the same as Pluxo at 600 minutes while giving 800 included minutes. Do not discount below ₹4,499 until real usage records prove margin. Offer a 14-day overflow-only pilot rather than a free month, and publish exactly what counts as a minute, transfer time and WhatsApp fees.

### Three one-line competitive pitches

- **Against Pluxo:** “Compare both on your real missed calls: Avery gives you 800 included minutes, Hindi/Hinglish, Google Calendar, DPDP consent records and a fixed ₹4,999 clinic bill.”
- **Against Practo/Clinicia/Eka:** “Keep the clinic software you already use—Avery answers the calls it cannot and sends the booking to your calendar and WhatsApp.”
- **Against an agency using Ringg/Bolna/Vapi/Retell:** “A demo bot is cheap; Avery includes the Indian number, dental workflow, consent, follow-up, monitoring and one accountable support team.”

### Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “We already use Practo.” | “Keep it. Practo is your diary and marketplace; Avery handles unanswered, busy and after-hours calls to your own number. Start with conditional forwarding and measure recovered bookings.” |
| “Our receptionist does this.” | “Avery is overflow and after-hours cover, not a replacement. Forward only busy/no-answer calls for 14 days and compare captured bookings with your missed-call log.” |
| “Too costly.” | “The Clinic plan is a fixed ₹4,999 before GST for up to 800 minutes. Run the pilot on missed calls only; keep it only if recovered bookings exceed the fee.” |

## 6. Product gaps ranked by likely deal loss

| Rank | Gap | Why it loses | One-line fix |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | No Practo/Clinicia/Eka sync | Fear of double booking or duplicate data blocks adoption. | Ship CSV/webhook export plus receptionist-confirm mode, then one two-way clinic-software connector. |
| 2 | Only three languages | Pluxo's 11-language claim is stronger in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai. | Add Kannada, Telugu and Tamil through Sarvam and publish call samples. |
| 3 | No Indian dental proof | Clinics trust peer outcomes more than architecture claims. | Run five overflow pilots and publish answer rate, bookings recovered and no-show change. |
| 4 | Weaker recall/no-show workflow | Pluxo sells revenue recovery, not just answering. | Add configurable recall, review and no-show WhatsApp sequences. |
| 5 | Existing-number setup uncertainty | Clinics will not change the number printed everywhere. | Publish tested Airtel/Jio/Vi/BSNL conditional-forwarding guides and a 30-minute activation SLA. |
| 6 | Unclear urgent-call escalation | Dental pain and emergencies require a safe human path. | Add urgent-keyword rules, warm transfer and WhatsApp escalation with an auditable outcome. |
| 7 | No booking deposit | High-value treatment bookings may still no-show. | Send a Razorpay deposit link after booking, with clinic-configured amount and refund text. |
