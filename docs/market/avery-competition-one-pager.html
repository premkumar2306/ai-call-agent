Five findings

  • Pluxo is the direct threat: same buyer, calls + WhatsApp, 11 languages and public pricing.
  • Practo is an installed workflow and integration target, not the product to replace.
  • Builders look cheaper only before clinic setup, testing and operating responsibility.
  • WhatsApp tools anchor low but do not recover missed calls.
  • Publish outcomes and a complete INR rate card before scaling sales.

Fix before deals are lost

  • Practo / Clinicia / Eka connector or safe confirmation mode.
  • Kannada, Telugu and Tamil call support with samples.
  • One live Indian dental case study with measured outcomes.
  • Recall, no-show and urgent-call workflows.
