## 2. Dental supply distributors

Distributors visit clinics every week and want things to bundle. Offer a **20% recurring referral
commission for 12 months** (draft), or a co-branded "free 14-day missed-call audit".

- Pinkblue.in (B2B dental supplies, strong in South India): partnerships page / sales team
- Dentalkart (Delhi; ships India-wide; runs dentist webinars): marketing team for a sponsored webinar
- Medikabazaar (Mumbai; serves clinics nationally)
- Local dealers: ask the first 3 pilot clinics "who supplies you?" and get a warm intro. That beats cold-calling distributors.

## 4. Dental college alumni groups

Young dentists opening their first clinic are the best early adopters. Reach them through alumni
associations, their Facebook/WhatsApp groups (join only through a member's invitation; don't cold-post) and
CDE days.
- Bengaluru: Government Dental College & Research Institute (Victoria campus); M R Ambedkar Dental College;
  The Oxford Dental College; Vokkaligara Sangha Dental College; KLE Society's Institute of Dental Sciences
- Hyderabad: Government Dental College & Hospital (Afzalgunj); Panineeya Institute of Dental Sciences;
  Army College of Dental Sciences (Secunderabad); Sri Sai College of Dental Surgery (Vikarabad).
  Full list: https://telanganastatedentalcouncil.in/dental-college.php
- Ask: a 15-minute "starting your clinic: tools that save a receptionist" slot at an alumni meet, or a
  post in their alumni newsletter.

## 6. Referral offer (for pilots and paying clinics)

- **Clinic → clinic:** refer a dentist who starts paying and **both get one month free**.
  Paid as a credit on the next Razorpay invoice.
- **Partner (distributor, software reseller, consultant):** 20% of the subscription fee for 12 months.
  *(Draft; owner to approve.)*
- Ask for the referral on day 10 of the pilot, when the clinic has seen its first "caught" bookings.
  Script: "Is there a dentist friend who has the same after-hours problem? I'll set them up free, and
  you both get a month off."
